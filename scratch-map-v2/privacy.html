Privacy policy

Scratch Map. Last updated: September 30, 2026.

Scratch Map is a small personal travel map. This page explains, in plain words, what it saves about you and what happens to it.

What we save

  • Your email address and a password, so you can sign in. Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form. Nobody can read your password, including the person who runs the app.
  • The places you scratch off: countries, US states, Canadian provinces, cities you type in, and any trip dates or notes you add.

We do not ask for, or save, your location, contacts, photos, or payment details.

Who can see it

Only you can see your places. The database is set up so every account can read and change only its own rows. The person who runs the app can technically open the database, and promises not to look at or share anyone's places.

Where it is kept

Your account and places are stored with Supabase, a database service, and the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. A copy of your places is also kept on your phone so the map opens with no signal. The map fonts load from Google Fonts, which may see your device's IP address when they load.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use trackers or analytics.
  • We do not share your places with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything yourself at any time. Open the app, go to My data, and tap Delete my account and data. This permanently removes your login and every place you saved. You can also download a copy of your places first from the same screen.

Children

Scratch Map is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top will change and the app will point you to the new version.
